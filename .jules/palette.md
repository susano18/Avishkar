## 2026-06-23 - [Improving Login Accessibility]
**Learning:** Standardizing label-input associations using `htmlFor` and `id` is crucial for screen reader compatibility, even in minimalist, custom-styled UIs. Explicit focus indicators (`focus-visible`) are essential for keyboard navigation when default browser outlines are suppressed.
**Action:** Always verify that every form input has a corresponding label with `htmlFor` and that interactive elements have clearly visible focus states for keyboard-only users.
